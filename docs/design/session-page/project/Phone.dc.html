The session

You're good at your job. You're less sure it's the right job.

We'll separate what you want from what you think you should want, and turn it into one move you can make this month.

Bring the thing you keep circling back to: the promotion you're not sure you want, the side project that won't go away, the manager conversation you keep postponing. We'll look at it from three angles (your values, your energy, your bank account) and leave with a decision you can live with, or a clear test to run before you make one.
Before your session

Once you book, I'll send a short questionnaire so we don't spend the first 10 minutes on background. Bring one situation you're stuck on.

You'll walk away with

  1. 1A short list of what you actually want from work, in your own words
  2. 2One decision made, or one clear test to run before you make it
  3. 3A first step for this week, small enough that you'll actually do it

I spent twelve years in HR watching brilliant women get passed over for roles they'd have been great at, mostly because nobody had helped them say out loud what they wanted. Now that's the whole job.

I work best with people who are ready to be honest about what isn't working. Sessions are practical: you'll leave with something to do, not just something to think about.
